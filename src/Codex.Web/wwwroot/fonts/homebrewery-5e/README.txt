The faces Homebrewery's 5e PHB theme names, for the book's Homebrewery compatibility layer (docs/BOOK_EXPORT.md 41.12).
Copied unchanged (renamed to lower case) from https://github.com/naturalcrit/homebrewery, themes/fonts/5e, commit 3e600a0.
They are Homebrewery's copies, not the originals below: Homebrewery's Scaly Sans, Scaly Sans Caps, Nodesto Caps Condensed and
Solbera Imitation Tweak have other widths and line metrics than the originals, and only theirs lay a page out as Homebrewery does.

Bookinsanity, Scaly Sans, Scaly Sans Caps, Mr Eaves Small Caps, Nodesto Caps Condensed, Nodesto Caps Wide, Solbera Imitation Tweak
  By Solbera, with fixes by Ryrok, additions by Ners ("Remake") and adjustments by LUCASTUCIOUS
  (collected at https://github.com/jonathonf/solbera-dnd-fonts), and changes by Homebrewery's contributors
  (re-exported, metrics and digit widths adjusted, the drop-cap face tweaked).
