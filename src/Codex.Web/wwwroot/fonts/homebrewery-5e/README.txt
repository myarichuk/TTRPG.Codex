The faces Homebrewery's 5e PHB theme names, for the book's Homebrewery compatibility layer (docs/BOOK_EXPORT.md 41.12).
All but one are copied unchanged (renamed to lower case) from https://github.com/naturalcrit/homebrewery, themes/fonts/5e, commit
3e600a0: Homebrewery's copies, not the originals, because Homebrewery's Scaly Sans, Scaly Sans Caps and Nodesto Caps Condensed have
other widths and line metrics than the originals, and only theirs lay a page out as Homebrewery does.

The drop-cap face is the exception: solbera-imitation.woff2 is Solbera Imitation 1.60 from https://github.com/jonathonf/solbera-dnd-fonts
(commit 1722376), converted from OTF to WOFF2 with fontTools and otherwise unchanged. Homebrewery ships an older, tweaked 1.30
("Solbera Imitation Tweak") whose own metadata reads "All Rights Reserved" and names no licence, so it is not shipped here; drop caps are
therefore a little different in size from Homebrewery's.

Bookinsanity, Scaly Sans, Scaly Sans Caps, Mr Eaves Small Caps, Nodesto Caps Condensed, Nodesto Caps Wide, Solbera Imitation
  By Solbera, with fixes by Ryrok, additions by Ners ("Remake") and adjustments by LUCASTUCIOUS
  (collected at https://github.com/jonathonf/solbera-dnd-fonts), and, for all but Solbera Imitation, changes by Homebrewery's
  contributors (re-exported, metrics and digit widths adjusted).
