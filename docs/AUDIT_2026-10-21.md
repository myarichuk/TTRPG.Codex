# TTRPG.Codex — Audit of 2026-10-21

*Snapshot of `main` @ `dab20cc`. Method: read every file under `src/`, `content/systems/srd5e/system.yaml`,
the importer and class tables, and samples of every SRD pack folder; built (`dotnet build`: 0 errors) and
ran the unit suite (`dotnet test` without E2E: 295/295 pass); ran the app against the seeded dev database
and walked every screen as the DM and as a player (desktop 1440px, phone 375px, light and dark). Line
numbers are from this commit. The previous audit is [AUDIT_2026-10-18.md](AUDIT_2026-10-18.md); items
from it that are still open are marked **(F14)** / **(F15)**.*

Four sections: **§1 UI** (why it reads as corporate rather than an RPG product, and UI/UX defects that
hold regardless of theme), **§2 functionality** (bugs, half-built flows, authorisation), **§3 rules**
(gaps between the engine and the 2024 rules / SRD 5.2.1), **§4 other issues** (concurrency, data,
tests, hygiene). §5 is a suggested order of work.

## 1. UI

### 1.1 Why it reads as "corporate"

The bar is Owlcat's Pathfinder UI: dark leather/stone/parchment materials, brass and bone accents, an
engraved serif display face, ornamental frames and dividers, illustrated icons for every game concept,
portrait frames, dense but legible stat plates. The current UI is a well-executed SaaS admin dashboard.
Every one of the choices below is a product-app idiom, and together they are the whole look:

Where an RPG product has to shine, the same idioms apply:

- **Character sheet** (`ActorSheet.razor`): a profile-picture squircle with a camera button; ability scores as KPI tiles (uppercase label, big modifier, a Bootstrap number input *inside* the tile); HP/AC as Bootstrap `display-6` numerals; spell slots as indigo pips plus "Use"/"Regain" `btn-sm`; inventory as a hover-list with a number input, an "Equipped" checkbox and a 14px ×; spells/feats as tag-input chips; the rest a settings form (`fieldset` + `.field-grid`). ~12 identical white panels in one scroll, no tabs, no sticky summary strip, no skills/saves/attacks/features anywhere on the page, no portrait frame, no class/species glyphs.
- **Combat tracker** (`CombatConsole.razor`): white rounded cards, name + four pills (initiative, state, red HP pill, conditions), an 8px bar, and for the DM ~9 inline form controls per combatant (Amount, Damage, Heal, Condition, Rounds, Add condition, Delay, Ready, Clear): with eight combatants it is a spreadsheet. No portraits/tokens; `ActorAvatar` and `ActorKind` are never used, so PCs, NPCs and monsters are identical rows. Dice = text field + "Roll" + two-line log rows. Current turn = gradient border + pulsing dot.
- **Library** (`LibraryBrowser`, `EntryDetail`): master/detail admin list with a gradient-filled active chip; entries render as a `<dl>` property list and markdown. No stat-block layout (banded Actions/Reactions, red rules), no spell-card header strip, no item card, no image slot.

### 1.2 UI/UX defects that hold regardless of theme

**Observed on screen**

- U1. **Library list is unbounded and unpaginated**: "Showing 150 of 1281 results – search to narrow", a flat alphabetical mix of rules-glossary entries ("Abjuration", "Abyssal", "Acid") with creatures and spells; the glossary swamps everything on the "All" chip. `LibraryBrowser.razor:23`.
- U2. **Monster rows show raw markdown** in the list summary (`*Large Aberration, Lawful Evil* AC 17 HP 150 …`) and the wiki card preview shows a literal `###`. `LibraryBrowser.razor:70`, `WikiSection.razor:260`.
- U3. **Non-casters get a "Prepared spells 0 of 0 — Limit reached" box** and an empty Cantrips box on the sheet (seen on a level-4 Fighter). The list panels should be hidden when `maxCount` resolves to 0 for every class. `EntryListPicker.razor:53`.
- U4. **The session event log shows raw enum names as badges** ("ResourceSpent", "Rest") and user GUIDs in descriptions ("took 5 HP damage from 3f2a…"). `SessionDetail.razor:186`, `RuntimeCommands.cs:22-26`.
- U5. **Status inconsistency after a restart**: Overview says "No session is running right now" while the Sessions list and session page show the same session as **Live** (the runtime died with the process, the document kept `Status = Live`). See F2/F4 in §2.
- U6. **Unknown workspace URLs silently render the Overview** (`/campaigns/{id}/anything`) instead of a not-found. `CampaignPage.razor:114`.
- U7. **Phone**: the campaign header (breadcrumb + title + two pills + the nav strip) takes the whole first screen before any content; the nav strip hides its scrollbar so "Settings" is off-screen with no cue; the sheet's vitals column (HP/AC/slots) stacks *below* the long form. `app.css:723-744`.
- U8. **Times are in the server's zone**: every timestamp uses `ToLocalTime()` on the server, so a remote player sees the DM's machine's clock (seen as 02:21 events for an evening session). 16 call sites, e.g. `SessionDetail.razor:185`.
- U9. **"Played by" dropdowns list raw user GUIDs.** `CastSection.razor:97-103`, `ActorSheet.razor:574-580`.
- U10. **Condition entry is a free-text box** with a hidden datalist and a Rounds spinner (min 1); no icons, no "until removed". `CombatConsole.razor:120-142`.

**Inconsistency (from the markup)**

- U11. Heading levels: `.panel-title` uses h2 in 20 places and h3 in 26; page headers are h1 on six pages and h2 on all twelve campaign sections; size is set with `.h4/.h5/.h6` utilities (46 uses) independent of level, so the same level renders at different sizes across pages. Empty-state headings are h1/h2/h3 depending on the page.
- U12. Four button vocabularies (`btn-*`, `icon-btn`, `btn-link-plain`, `btn-link`) and **six different "remove" affordances** (trash icon-btn, × icon-btn, × link-plain, outline-danger "Remove", outline-danger "Delete", a `.pill.pill-button`). `btn-outline-primary` appears once in the app (SlotTracker "Long rest").
- U13. Save model: the sheet has one "Save changes" at the top for ~12 panels with no dirty indicator, no sticky bar, no unload guard, and two panels tell you in prose to remember to save (`ActorSheet.razor:500,895`); SessionDetail has four separate save buttons in two styles; Settings mixes `btn-primary` and `btn-outline-secondary btn-sm` saves.
- U14. Two search idioms (toolbar with icon vs bare `form-control-sm type=search`), two filter idioms (chips vs selects), two add-to-initiative flows (select + "Roll 1d20" / "or" / number + "Set", plus a separate Quick-add panel). Bestiary's search uses `.search` outside `.toolbar`, so its icon is unpositioned. `BestiarySection.razor:126`.
- U15. Dialog vs inline is arbitrary: New campaign / Create character / Import are dialogs; New session / region / fact / provider / journal entry are inline forms. Primary actions sit top-right, in a right-column panel, or at the bottom of a panel depending on page.
- U16. Bootstrap grid leaks on 12 pages with three different splits (8/4, 7/5, 6/6) and a breakpoint hack for the side rail (`app.css:720-722`). Short inputs sized five ways (`.w-5 !important`, `.count-input`, inline `style="width:6rem"`, `max-width`, `w-auto`). 240 `mb-*` utilities.
- U17. Icons: edit = `edit-3` ×7 / `pen-line` ×1; `map` is the Campaigns nav, the Places section and the no-campaigns illustration; three book glyphs for Lore/Library/Wiki; nine icon sizes; about half the panel titles have an icon.

**Interaction**

- U18. `HpBadge` always emits `pill-danger`, so a full-HP combatant wears a red badge. `CombatConsole.razor:464`.
- U19. Condition remove, inventory remove, spell/feat remove are 12–14px `btn-link-plain` with `padding:0`; `.btn-sm` is ~30px tall; the initiative row packs five per creature. Below any touch guideline. `app.css:307,549`.
- U20. Tooltips are native `title=` on six controls (invisible to touch and keyboard); no tooltip component, so no rules text on hover anywhere.
- U21. Errors go to one top-of-page flash; on a 2000px sheet it renders off-screen with no scroll or focus move, and a second flash replaces the first. Rules violations are a bullet list at the top; only attributes get an inline marker.
- U22. Under 720px nav links become icon-only with no `aria-label`. `AppHeader.razor:13-23`.
- U23. "End the table" and journal delete have no confirmation; every other destructive action does. `CombatConsole.razor:206`, `JournalSection.razor:140`.
- U24. Home cards: a stretched link covers the whole card *and* an "Open" button; the delete button needs `z-index` to be clickable. `app.css:366-369`.
- U25. `LoadingSkeleton` always draws three title+sub rows regardless of what it replaces, so layout still jumps. Selecting a Library entry collapses the list from `col-12` to `col-lg-5` on first click.

### 1.3 What would move it furthest (in order)

1. **Dark-first material tokens**: near-black stone `--bg`, leather/parchment `--surface`, two-tone brass + inner-dark borders instead of 10% hairlines, brass/gold `--accent`, bone `--accent-2`, blood-red HP, verdigris for magic. Delete the 13 gradient uses, the body wash, the auth blobs, all `backdrop-filter`. Light becomes an opt-in "parchment" theme. Update `manifest.webmanifest`, `offline.html`, `icons/icon.svg`.
2. **Two self-hosted faces**: an engraved/serif display for h1–h3, brand, ability/HP/AC numerals; a readable body face. Positive tracking, `font-variant: small-caps` for labels; retire the uppercase-eyebrow recipe and `display-6`.
3. **Frames, not cards**: rewrite `.panel`, `.campaign-card`, `.participant`, `.list-picker`, dialogs with 0–3px radius, double-rule/bevelled borders, corner ornaments from a small SVG sprite, a titled header band; replace pills with plaques/chamfers; ornamental dividers instead of `hr`/row hairlines.
4. **Own the controls**: `CodexButton` (plaque / etched / blood / rune-icon) and `CodexField`, migrate `ActorSheet` and `CombatConsole` first, then retire the ~270 direct Bootstrap control usages; one "remove" affordance.
5. **Sheet re-layout**: sticky plate header (framed portrait, name in display type, class/level/species plaques, stat strip: HP + temp + death saves, AC shield, initiative, speed, proficiency); engraved ability plates with score large and modifier in a medallion and a breakdown tooltip; tabs for Attributes / Picks / Inventory / History / Notes; sticky save bar with a dirty count. Add the missing skills/saves/attacks/features sections (see §3).
6. **Encounter board**: portrait token with kind-coloured frame per row, initiative plaque, one HP bar with numeric overlay, condition icon strip with tooltips; a single selected-combatant command panel (±1/±5, damage type, condition picker, delay/ready) instead of nine controls per row; round/turn banner; sticky current-turn callout; confirm on End.
7. **Dice tray**: per-die faces, kept/dropped dice, nat 20/1 emphasis, total in display type, roll buttons on the sheet (attack / save / check with the sheet's modifiers).
8. **Domain icon set** (classes, species, conditions, damage types, schools, item categories, creature types, roles); Lucide only for chrome; three sizes; heraldic placeholders instead of initials/blobs.
9. **Library as reference cards**: creature stat block, spell card, item card layouts; load-more; stable detail-pane width; homebrew editor in the detail pane; hide the glossary from "All".
10. **Consistency pass**: one heading scheme, one grid, one empty-state component with in-world copy, a tooltip component, a fixed toast region, one `ConfirmDialog` in `MainLayout`, fix U9/U18/U19/U22.

## 2. Functionality

### Critical

- **F1. Campaign import lets any DM take over or overwrite any campaign by id.** `CampaignExportService.ImportAsync` (`CampaignExportService.cs:77-97`) authorises against the *uploaded* bundle's `OwnerId`/`Members`, then saves the campaign and upserts every actor/session/note/fact/region/homebrew/wiki document carrying that campaign id. `SettingsSection.razor:359-386` never requires `bundle.Campaign.Id == Ctx.Campaign.Id`. A user who is DM of their own throwaway campaign can craft a JSON bundle with `Campaign.Id = <victim>` and `OwnerId = <self>` and become owner of someone else's campaign, or inject/overwrite its actors, sessions and DM notes. The only test checks strangers relative to the bundle. *Fix: resolve access against the stored document when one exists (require DM; keep stored owner/members); accept the bundle's membership only when no such campaign exists; in the UI require the id to match unless "restore as new" is chosen.*
- **F2. Lost updates between the character sheet and the live table, in both directions.** The runtime hydrates a copy of each `ActorDocument` at start (`CampaignRuntime.cs:98-107`) and `PersistDirtyAsync` (`:239-259`) stores that **whole cached document**; `NextTurnCommand` marks **every** actor dirty (`RuntimeCommands.cs:177`), so each "Next turn" rewrites every actor from the runtime's stale copy. `ActorRepository.SaveAsync` is a plain `StoreAsync` with no concurrency check (`RavenActorRepository.cs:48-60`). Meanwhile the sheet's `TrySaveAsync` replaces Sheet/State/Name/Biography/DmNotes with its own stale copy. Manifestations: a player's level-up, spell picks or the DM's notes during a session are reverted on the next turn; a player who opens their sheet, takes damage, then saves writes the pre-damage HP back. **Observed**: the Cast and Overview pages showed Aria at 27/34 while the live table and the sheet showed 7/34, because `PersistDirtyAsync` updates `State` but not `Sheet`, and the lists read `Sheet`. *Fix: route sheet saves through the runtime while it is live (a `ReplaceActorCommand`), patch only `State` from the runtime, and mirror resources back to `Sheet` on persist.*
- **F3. The session document is overwritten from stale copies by both the runtime and the session page.** The runtime keeps `_activeSession` and `StoreAsync`es it after every command (`CampaignRuntime.cs:265-274`); `SessionDetail` saves its own copy from page open (`SessionDetail.razor:273-296`). While the table is live, "Recap saved" is overwritten by the next dice roll, and saving a recap/DM summary deletes every event, roll and note appended since the page opened. The Phase 4 scenario saves the recap only after the table ended. *Fix: field-level patches for Recap/DmSummary/Title/Status and append-only patches for events/rolls, or route session edits through the runtime.*

### High

- **F4. Only the first session can ever be live.** `GetOrOpenLiveSessionAsync` (`CampaignRuntimeManager.cs:82-109`) reuses `CurrentSessionId` forever and sets it only when null; nothing else writes it. Planned sessions can never be started; "Start the table" after session 1 appends session 2's events into session 1 and flips it back to Live. (Also the cause of U5.)
- **F5. Deleting a campaign does not stop its runtime** (`CampaignRepository.DeleteAsync:84-113` never evicts); the next command at the still-live table resurrects actors and the session as orphans. There is still no idle eviction **(F14)**, only the manual End button.
- **F6. Hidden actors and exact monster damage leak to players through the session event log.** Command descriptions use real names and exact numbers (`RuntimeCommands.cs:22-26,150-154,196-203,335-339`) and `SessionDetail.razor:182-187` renders every event to every member, while the live table carefully hides hidden participants and shows HP bands only. The player-facing AI recap digest consumes the same events. *Fix: store actor ids on events and redact per viewer; filter the player digest.*
- **F7. Library stat blocks are reachable by players.** `LibraryBrowser` hides non-`PlayerFacing` types from the chip row and the search results (`:168,186-190`), but `GetAsync` (`:199-208`) applies no filter, so `/campaigns/{id}/library/monster/{id}` opens any creature (including the DM's homebrew creatures). **Observed live as the player**: the detail page returned the Adult Red Dragon stat block, and the "All" list still contained 56 creature rows.
- **F8. Whole-document campaign saves from stale copies drop members and revert the current session.** `SettingsSection.razor:251-339` and `BestiarySection.razor:217-236` save the `CampaignDocument` loaded at page open; invites (`JoinByInviteCodeAsync`) and the runtime (`CurrentSessionId`) write the same document. A DM who opens Settings, has a player join, then saves anything silently removes that player. No optimistic concurrency anywhere.
- **F9. The encounter is never restored, cannot be ended, and participants cannot be removed.** `GetOrStartAsync` never loads the persisted `EncounterDocument`; `_activeEncounter` starts null, so End + Start (or a restart) wipes the initiative order. No `EndEncounter`/`RemoveParticipant` command; dead monsters stay in the order. Conditions are deliberately not persisted (`PluginLoader.cs:80-87`) and vanish on End/restart with no warning **(F14)**.
- **F10. Actors added while the table is live are not hydrated.** Only `QuickAddMonsterAsync` enqueues `HydrateActorCommand`; `CastSection.CreateAsync`, `CreateWithAiAsync` and `CastService.AddToCastAsync` (Bestiary "To cast", Library "Add to cast") save the document only. Such a creature can be added to initiative but `ApplyDamageCommand` finds no entity and silently does nothing.

### Medium

- F11. `ActorVisibility.Unknown` ("seen but not identified") is dead: players are excluded from Unknown actors exactly like Hidden (`RavenActorRepository.cs:38-44,170-171`), so the `PublicName`/`PublicDescription` UI never reaches anyone.
- F12. Initiative: the UI never passes `TieBreak`, so ties fall to GUID order; re-setting a participant's initiative re-sorts without adjusting `TurnIndex`, so the current turn jumps; Delay/Ready are labels only; `HasActed` is never shown and `PreviousTurnCommand` does not restore it. `CombatConsole.razor:402,413`, `CampaignRuntime.cs:149-162`, `RuntimeCommands.cs:134-148`.
- F13. Render-time enumeration of the runtime's live collections (`encounter.Participants`, `RollLog`, `Notes`) from the circuit thread while the command loop mutates them on its own thread (`CombatConsole.razor:57,162,581-603`); a collision throws inside render and kills that player's circuit. Publish immutable snapshots with `StateChanged`.
- F14. Double subscription on start: `StartOrJoinAsync` attaches via the `RuntimeChanged` callback and again via `Attach(...)`; `DisposeAsync` unsubscribes once, leaking the component until eviction. `CombatConsole.razor:341-390`.
- F15. Cascade deletes query without `WaitForNonStaleResults` in seven repositories (actors, sessions, notes, regions, facts, homebrew, encounters), so documents written seconds before deletion survive as orphans.
- F16. Export/import round trip is incomplete: attachments (covers, portraits) are not exported but `HasCover`/`HasPortrait` are; `WorldState`, fact/location `Metadata` and `ActiveEffects` arrive as `JsonElement` (the code's own comment says Raven persists that as garbage). Settings copy promises a full restore.
- F17. Homebrew `EntryId` uniqueness is not enforced (`RavenHomebrewRepository.TrySaveAsync:34-81`); duplicates resolve to whichever is first in shortlist links, sheet picks and import-skip logic.
- F18. `CampaignRole.Observer` is unimplemented: every check is `IsDm`, so an Observer can create a PC, roll, add notes and keep a journal, yet it is offered in the Settings role dropdown.
- F19. AI calls have no timeout or cancellation except the settings "Test connection"; a stalled provider leaves "Drafting…" forever and the handler runs on after navigation. `AiAssistant.cs:18-50,159-174`.
- F20. Sessions cannot be deleted; the date cannot be edited; the Status select lets the DM mark Live with no runtime, or Completed while the runtime flips it back (F3) **(F15 partly open)**.
- F21. Table quick notes and the roll log are invisible after the session (`SessionDetail` renders `NoteDocument`s and `Events`, never `RollLog`/`Notes`) and absent from the AI digest; secret rolls can never be revealed (`IsSecret` is never flipped). Two parallel note systems with different visibility models.
- F22. Rests at the table touch only spell slots; nothing grants temporary HP although damage honours a `TempHP` pool; no death saves, no concentration (see §3).
- F23. Condition durations: Rounds `min="1"` with no "until removed"; `EffectExpiry` is ignored by `CodexWorld.AdvanceTurn` so Start/End-of-turn is dead; the anchor is always the affected actor.
- F24. Lore and World are append-only: no edit/delete for facts, regions or locations; reveals are irreversible (facts, locations, shared wiki pages). `LocationDocument.Connections`/`TravelTimeMinutes` and `KnowledgeIndex` are never queried although `CAMPAIGN_ARCHITECTURE.md` presents them as the design.
- F25. Backups: a daily full backup is scheduled with no retention and no in-app restore (`RavenDbService.cs:107-125`).
- F26. Portrait upload/remove saves the entire sheet as a side effect (`ActorSheet.razor:991-1017`), committing unsaved edits.

### Low

- F27. Dead or unwired: the `/loading` route and `LoadingScreen` (loading completes before `app.Run()`), `ReconnectModal.razor.js` (duplicated by `app-startup.js`), `ContentRegistry.ExecuteAbilityAsync` + `ScriptEvaluator` (the ability pipeline in the architecture doc is never exercised at the table), `CodexWorld.Tick/AddSystem`, `AdvanceTurnCommand`, `StatModifierComponent`, `TagsComponent`, `ActorReferenceComponent`, `CampaignDocument.WorldState`, `EncounterDocument.ActiveEffects/LocationId`, `window.codexDownload`.
- F28. Roller names at the table: anyone but you and the owner is "Player"; rolls are never tied to an actor (`ActorId` always null). `CombatConsole.razor:573,587-588`.
- F29. Damage input is not cleared after Damage/Heal; the condition input is. `CombatConsole.razor:504-514`.
- F30. `/login/external` accepts cookie scheme names (harmless bounce). `ExternalLoginPolicy.cs:32-40`.
- F31. Stale reads right after writes in `RavenUserRepository` username/external-login queries; two DMs starting the table concurrently can create two sessions. `CampaignRuntimeManager.cs:48-65`.
- F32. Dice typos throw `FormatException` inside `Apply` and are logged as errors by the loop; validate before enqueueing. `CampaignRuntime.cs:227-231`.
- F33. Wiki note deletion is authorised only in Razor; `RavenNoteRepository.DeleteNoteAsync` takes no `CampaignAccess`.
- F34. The service-worker shell list omits `Codex.Web.styles.css`, so the offline page is partly unstyled.

### What a DM running a 5e campaign would expect and cannot do yet

Run a second session (plan one and start it) · end an encounter, remove a dead creature, reorder or break ties, act on a delay/ready · let players roll initiative/attacks for their own character with their modifiers and advantage, reveal a secret roll, review roll history · track XP or milestones, loot and currency, death saves, concentration, temp HP, rests that heal HP and spend hit dice · indefinite conditions that survive ending the table · "seen but not identified" NPCs under a public name · assign a PC to a player by name · edit or un-reveal lore, places and shared wiki pages · maps or handouts, chat/whispers, notifications to players not on the Table page · restore a backup or an export with its images from inside the app.

## 3. Rules: engine vs. 2024 rules / SRD 5.2.1

What *is* modelled matches the 2024 rules well, and was verified line by line: point buy (27 / 8–15 / costs), standard array, cap 20, background +2/+1 or +1/+1/+1 restricted to the background's abilities, proficiency bonus, HP (full die at 1, die/2+1 after, min 1 per level, CON retroactive, per-class die when multiclassing), the 12 class spell/slot/prepared/cantrip tables, pact slot count and level, the multiclass slot table and prerequisites, ASI/boon feat levels, boon cap 30, light/medium(+2)/heavy(0) dex caps, shield +2, Barbarian/Monk Unarmored Defense, Defense style only with armor, the 15 conditions, and `adv`/`dis`/`kh`/`kl` dice. The gaps are mostly in what is not modelled, plus a few real bugs.

### 3.1 Modelled wrong

- **R1 (High). Fighting Style feats collide with ASI slots; a level-1 Fighter cannot save under strict rules.** `system.yaml:114-123` puts `fighting-style` feats in the same `feats` list whose limit is `featSlots.slots`, and the Fighter's level-1 row is `slots: 0` (`classes/fighter.yaml:152`). Picking Defense at level 1 yields "1 picked, but only 0 allowed" (`SheetRuleEngine.cs:208-211`). Paladin 2 / Ranger 2 / Champion 7 have the same problem. The only test asserts AC, not the violation. *Fix: a separate fighting-style list with its own slot column, or a per-category allowance inside `featSlots`.* (Confirmed on screen: Fighter 4 shows "Feats 0 of 1", where the rules give a style plus an ASI.)
- **R2 (High). Initiative ignores Dexterity** (and Alert, Incapacitated).
