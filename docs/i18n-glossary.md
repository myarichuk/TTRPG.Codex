## Forged in the Dark SRD (fitd-srd)

**Machine-drafted, for native review.** These are the terms used in `content/systems/fitd-srd/system.ru.yaml`, `system.he.yaml` and the packs
`srd-fitd-2017-ru` / `srd-fitd-2017-he`. They are our own choices, not taken from any published translation, and no published edition is
claimed to agree with them. The system name stays "Forged in the Dark SRD" in every language (a title). A native reviewer can check every
name in this one table. The rule names (`rule` entries) follow the same words; only free text is translated, never `category`, `order`,
`attribute`, `cost`, `source` or `tags`. Numbers, `+1d`, `1d6`-style dice and bold keywords keep their form (the bold word is translated).
